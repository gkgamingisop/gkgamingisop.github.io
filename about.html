📜   GK GAMING

Server Rules.

Welcome to GK Gaming! A place to game, chill, hang out, make friends and have a good time. Keep these rules in mind.

01. Respect Everyone

Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, bullying, targeted personal attacks or unnecessary toxicity.

02. No Discrimination

Racism, sexism, hate speech or discrimination based on someone's identity will not be tolerated.

03. No Spamming

Avoid message flooding, excessive emojis, repeated messages, unwanted pings and excessive caps.

04. No NSFW Content

Pornography, explicit sexual content and inappropriate gore are strictly prohibited.

05. No Unnecessary Drama

Avoid creating unnecessary conflicts, starting arguments or bringing personal drama into the community.

06. Use the Correct Channels

Keep conversations relevant to their respective channels. Avoid derailing discussions or flooding unrelated channels.

07. Voice Chat Etiquette

No mic spamming, ear-rape audio, soundboard abuse or intentionally disrupting voice conversations.

08. No Scamming or Malicious Links

Phishing, suspicious downloads, scams, token grabbers and malicious links are strictly prohibited.

09. Respect Staff Decisions

Follow moderator instructions. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it privately and respectfully.

10. No Impersonation

Do not impersonate staff, other members or public figures to mislead others.

11. Mild Swearing Is Allowed

Casual swearing, friendly banter and jokes are perfectly fine. However, excessive abusive language, targeted insults, harassment and threats are not allowed.

12. Have Fun

This is a chill gaming and hangout community. Make friends, play games, have conversations and enjoy yourself. Nobody is forced to be active.

⚖️   RULE ENFORCEMENT

Actions Against Violations.

Consequences of Breaking Rules

Depending on the severity and frequency of violations, staff may issue:

Verbal warning
Official warning
Timeout
Kick
Temporary ban
Permanent ban

Staff reserve the right to take appropriate action against behaviour that disrupts the community, even if it is not explicitly listed above.

By remaining in GK Gaming, you agree to follow these rules. Enjoy your stay! 🎮
