Server Rules.

Help us keep GK Gaming friendly, enjoyable and welcoming for everyone.

01. Respect Everyone

Treat all members with respect. Harassment, bullying and targeted insults are not allowed.

02. No Discrimination

Discrimination or hate speech based on race, religion, nationality, gender or identity is prohibited.

03. No Spamming

Avoid flooding chats, excessive mentions or unnecessary advertisements.

04. No NSFW Content

Explicit, sexual or otherwise inappropriate content is not allowed.

05. No Unnecessary Drama

Avoid provoking arguments, starting conflicts or bringing unnecessary drama.

06. Use the Correct Channels

Keep conversations in their appropriate channels.

07. Voice Chat Etiquette

Avoid mic spamming, disruptive noises and intentionally disturbing others.

08. No Scamming or Malicious Links

Scams, phishing links and malicious files are strictly prohibited.

09. Respect Staff Decisions

Follow staff instructions. Raise disagreements respectfully.

10. No Impersonation

Do not impersonate other members, staff or individuals.

11. Mild Swearing Is Allowed

Casual swearing and friendly banter are fine. Targeted abuse, harassment and threats are not.

12. Have Fun

Enjoy gaming, conversations and hanging out. Help make the community enjoyable.

Rule Enforcement.

Breaking the rules may result in disciplinary action depending on the seriousness and frequency of the offence.

  • Verbal warning
  • Official warning
  • Timeout
  • Kick from the server
  • Temporary ban
  • Permanent ban

Staff reserve the right to act against disruptive behaviour even if it is not explicitly listed above. By remaining in GK Gaming, you agree to follow these rules.
